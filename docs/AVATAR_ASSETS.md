# 人物头像素材

九位人物使用内置 **imagegen** 工具分别生成，已复制到项目 `web/avatars/`，运行时全部从本机加载。原始生成文件保留在工具默认的 `generated_images` 目录；项目无需访问该目录。

## 统一生成提示词

提示词采用以下视觉约束，并为每个人物单独指定形象特征：

> Create one square premium Chinese historical editorial portrait for a Three Kingdoms social deduction game. Semi-realistic painted face with delicate ink texture and a matte finish. Jade, ivory and muted gold palette; deep forest green background with a soft halo. Large centered head and shoulders, complete hairstyle and crown inside the frame, recognizable at 56px. No text, lettering, watermark, border, modern clothing or UI elements.
